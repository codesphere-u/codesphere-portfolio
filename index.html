Signature Architectural Decisions & Engineering Rigor

Why every repository prioritizes deterministic invariants, threat modeling, and reproducible benchmarks over fragile black-box wrappers.

Why Cyber Dragon Refuses to Fetch URLs

URL scanners that perform outbound HTTP fetches become SSRF proxies and trigger attacker tracking beacons. Cyber Dragon scores phishing domains, IDN homographs, and predatory USSD strings (*165#, *185#, *334#) 100% lexically in 0.28 ms with zero network egress.

Why FlowLedger Bans IEEE-754 Floats

Floating-point arithmetic silently drops cents and shillings during fee splits. FlowLedger enforces bigint minor-unit double-entry postings across UGX, KES, and USD with SHA-256 hash-chained journal entries (97 entries verified with 0 imbalance).

Why Auralis Binds Only to 127.0.0.1 with Single-Use Tokens

Local AI workspaces that bind to 0.0.0.0 expose developer filesystems to LAN and DNS-rebinding attacks. Auralis enforces strict loopback binding, symlink-safe realpath jails, and single-use HMAC approval tokens while indexing at 3,110 docs/sec.

2. Deterministic Invariants & Safety Boundaries

  • ✓ Zero outbound HTTP/DNS requests when analyzing user-submitted URLs—eliminating SSRF, internal port scanning, and attacker beacon callbacks.
  • ✓ Every risk verdict includes human-readable evidence signals and concrete remediation steps tailored for non-technical users.
  • ✓ Sub-millisecond execution (0.28 ms mean latency) enables real-time client-side scanning on low-end mobile devices.

3. Key Engineering Trade-Offs (Architecture Decision Records)

ADR-01: Refusal to Fetch User-Submitted URLs (SSRF-Free by Construction)

CONTEXT: Many URL scanners fetch the target page to inspect DOM content, inadvertently turning the scanner into an SSRF proxy and tipping off attackers via unique tracking URLs.

DECISION: Perform 100% lexical, structural, Shannon-entropy, Levenshtein brand-distance, and USSD/SMS pattern analysis without making any DNS or HTTP requests.

TRADE-OFFS: Cannot inspect post-redirect DOM payloads behind neutral shorteners without user expansion, but completely eliminates SSRF vulnerabilities, attacker canary triggers, and network latency.

VERIFICATION: Verified across 21 automated tests (TypeScript + FastAPI) and an 8/8 real-world threat corpus benchmark completing in 0.28 ms mean latency.
